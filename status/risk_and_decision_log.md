## Decision criteria at the next checkpoint

- **Continue candidate:** physical hinge progress and grasp+torque persistence
  increase without pathological contact/force spikes.
- **Promising candidate:** repeatable nonzero Stage 3 -> 4 transitions under the
  matched diagnostic, followed by evidence that Stage 4/5 training remains
  reachable.
- **Review/stop candidate:** shaping rewards rise while hinge angle and
  transition rate remain flat, or either P0 audit fails.
- **Long-horizon promotion blocked:** runtime telemetry does not confirm strict
  persistence reset and curriculum-scaled bridge behavior.
- **No conclusion:** job merely starts, checkpoint identities differ, reset-state
  distributions are not comparable, or the diagnostic protocol changes.
